## Perfect(adj)
1.  as good as it is possible to be
	- Life certainly isn't perfect at the moment
2. absolute; complete
	- A perfect stranger
3. Натуральное число, равное сумме всех своих собственных делителей
-----------
## Impeccable(adj)
1.  in accordance with the highest standards
	- That engine is impeccable!
-----------------------------------
Perfect ≈ Impeccable
