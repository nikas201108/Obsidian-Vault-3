## Nonsense(noun)
1. spoken or written words that have no meaning or make no sense.
	- He is spoked nonesense
2. foolish or unacceptable behaviour
	- She's a strong woman who stands no nonsense
-----------
## Gibberish(noun)
1.  unintelligible or meaningless speech or writing
	- He talks gibberish
-----------------------------------
Gibberish ≈ Nonsense
