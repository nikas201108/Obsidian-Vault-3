That is [[Verbs of possibility and ability|verbs of possibility and ability]]. 

Can/could — general ability. Can be used in past and present tenses.

- I coul- I can speak english d write carefully, before I went to university

Be able to/will be able to/may be able to — succesfull in one case. All tenses.
- I will be able to apply for that job!
- The thief was able to get in the shop.

Managed to —  end something with big efforts.
- He managed to end fiscal crisis of whole country!
