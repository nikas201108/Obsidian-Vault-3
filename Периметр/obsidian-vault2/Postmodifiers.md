Postmodifer that is construction in [[English]] with a same meaning like [[Premodifiers|premodifers]], which allow us to determine word more exactly. That follows a word or phrase.

We can highlight types of postmodifiers:
- A preposition with prepositional phrase
 The boy in the garden...
 - A dependent clause
 That was a best perfomance, which wasn't be alike others.
 - Adjectives
 ... and other things interesting
 - Non-Finite Postmodifying Clauses.

## Non-Finite Postmodifying Clauses
There are constructions like:
- A letter written by a member of the public
- I had a little bit to eat

There are can include -ing, -ed and _to_ verbs.
-ing and -ed and in rarely cases _to_ verbs we can detect by [[Substitution|substitution]].
A letter written by a member of the public → A letter, which was written by a member of the public.
