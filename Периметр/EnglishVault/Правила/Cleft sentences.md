#Rules
If we want emphasise some element from text, such as date, person, thing or place, we will use that thing. 

Two main cases:
1. With **it**. It + be + who/that(common). 
	- It was you who invited me to the party yesterday
	- It was the party that you invited me yesterday
2. With *what*. What/When/How/etc sentence + to be + noun or noun + to be + What sentence.
	- What I like best about learning history is making links between our current life and past.
	- How many money was spent is secret.
	- We write our project is what the most important work in my life.
