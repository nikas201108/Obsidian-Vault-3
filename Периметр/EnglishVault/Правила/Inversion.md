#Rules
If we want to emphasise some adverb(usually rarely, never, sooner) we can inverse sentence
- I have rarely read such wonderful story → Rarely have I read such wonderful story
- We rarely speak about exams → Rarely do we speak about exams

### Extra
Можно использовать с so/such…that:
- So bewildered was he that he left the room

Наречия места и направления тоже можно инвертировать:
- Down the stairs he ran

As soon as можно заменить с помощью No sooner + extra clause (inv) + than main clause:
- No sooner did I see him than I realised we had met before (As son as I saw him, I realised we had met before) 

Не только, но и:
- Not only did his wife leave him, but he also lost his job

Можно изменить under any circumstances и поставить в начало:
- Under no circumstances should you smoke

Anywhere передвинуть в начало, заменив на nowhere:
- Nowhere could we find a parking place (We couldn't find a parking place anywhere)

Ни на одну секунду/moment:
- Not for one second did she hesitate (she didn't hesistate for one second)

Until и отрицание в придаточной части эквивалентно:
- Not until he reached home did he stop running (He didn't stop running, until he reached home)

Не то, не то
- I am not hungry, nor am I thirsty (I'm not hungry and I am not thirsty either)
