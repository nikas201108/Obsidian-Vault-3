Main feature is contained in this fact: 

| Past be          | Present be                           | Verb                      |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------- |
| were in any case | be in any case                       | standard form in any case |
| If I were you…   | The police insisted the car be moved | I suggest he stop         |

### Present subjunctive 
We use with verbs demand, insist, suggest, require, request, recommend, propose, think:
- They demanded that he leave at once
- The school Principal suggested that he be awarded a scholarship

We use with adjectives important, essential and other:
- It is essential that she be at the meeting

We use it in sustainable forms:
- God save the Queen!
- Be that it may…
- Come what may…

If need be:
- If need be we can always bring another car

### Past subjunctive
- If the minister were here, he would no doubt refute the allegations
- I wouldn't argue with her if I were you
