
## With more

|              | Big difference                                        | Small difference            |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| **Formal**   | considerably, infinitely, decidedly,<br>significantly | marginally                  |
| **Neutral**  | much, far, a lot, a good deal                         | slightly, a bit, barely any |
| **Informal** | miles, way, loads                                     |                             |

The technology was considerably more advanced than ours
We're way better than you at football

## With as…as

| Big difference             | Small difference | No difference      |
| -------------------------- | ---------------- | ------------------ |
| nothing like, nowhere near | almost or nearly | just,<br>every bit |
He's nothing like as smart as his brother
He's almost as smart as his brother
He's every bit as smart as his brother

## Qualify
We can use qualifiers (three times, ten times) in comparative:
- It took three times as long as we thought it would
- They are ten times richer than us

But we can use half and twice only with as…as:
- It took twice as long as we though it would

## Clause
### Omitting in clause
To avoid repetition in comparative we can reduce it or use auxiliary verb
- We arrived earlier than (we) expected
- We arrived earlier than they did (arrived)
### Inversion
We can use inversion in clause with as (but can't with like):
- She hopes to study at Yale, as did her mum

## Article
With as…as we use article before noun:
- It is as exciting a story as she thinks
- It isn't as exciting a story as she thinks

But with negative + such as…as we put article before adjective:
- It isn't such an exciting story as she thinks
