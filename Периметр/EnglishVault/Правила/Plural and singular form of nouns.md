## Nouns end in -ics
They are usually not plural.
- Politics is an important part of society

## Sum of money, distance and time
Such sums are singular
- Three years is a long time duration

## Plural nouns used with singular
Latin words such as data, media, criteria, phenomena 
- All the data is available for public 

## Collective nouns
Грубо говоря, если речь идёт о конкретных индивидах в коллективе, то используем plural. Если говорим о цельном объекте, то singular
