#Rules
have something done = get something done
Actions, which we arrange for the people to do for us.
I had my car washed(someone wash that)
I get my homework done(someone do that)

Get someone to do(попросить кого-то сделать что-то, заплатить кому-то за услугу и т.п.)
She got bank to accept that transaction

Have somebody done. Что-то типа have something done, но акцент на деятели
I had Jack washed my car.
