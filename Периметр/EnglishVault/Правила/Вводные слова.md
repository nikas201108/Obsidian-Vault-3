### Ввиду
In view of:
- This is especially apparent **in view of** contemporary theatre
- **In view of** the fact that you have been ill, I'll give you more time to do your essay

---

### Потому что
Seeing as/that:
- **Seeing as** the weather isn't very good, I think we should change our plans for the weekend
- **Seeing that** it's pouring rain and all, how about we reschedule the picnic?

What with:
- I'm sorry, but I can't help you move this weekend, **what with** my bad back and all
- It's been a really busy week, **what with** revising for exams and my family moving house

Given that:
- **Given that** you usually get good marks, I don't understand why you failed 

---
### Благодаря
Owing to (только отрицательные):
- **Owing to bad weather**, this morning flight will be delayed

Due to:
- The restaurant's success is **due largely to** its new manager

---
### Принимая во внимание
Bearing in mind
- **Bearing in mind** the above-described problems, the invention was made.
