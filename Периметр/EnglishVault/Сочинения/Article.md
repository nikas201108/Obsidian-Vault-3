Urgency culture is rapidly broaden out worldwide and become new sad reality for a thousands of employees due to skyrocketing competition and communication technologies. Its influence is hard to overestimate now, but soon this „new order“ may be almost impossible to distinguish from ordinary life. Notably, it is directly related to the loss of importance of prioritisation skills and downfall of personal privacy as if the job was only thing in the world. Scarcely someone can call that acceptable except for executive management. Unfortunately, urgency culture is accompanied by other serious repercussions too such as heightened alertness, stress, anxiety according to psychologist Joel Frank. It is these issues that form vicious circle, harming people. Permanent impact of  „fight or fight“ instincts and overstimulation is catalyst for the decline of joy, plunge of reflective thinking and rise of hypertension, sleep deprivation, high cholesterol, inflammatory and blood pressure disorders . Sadly, there are more losses than benefits. We will not be able to overcome natural body limits and become a machine: there are no cases, when person can efficiently simultaneously solve a bunch of task as Friederike Fabritius said. The scar of the current style of life are being left on the current young generation especially and there is nothing we can do, while employers consider this the norm
