$$\begin{aligned}&O_1 B \perp BC\\\\& O_1A \perp AD \\\\& AD \parallel BC\end{aligned}\ \ \ \ \ \Longrightarrow O_1A\parallel O_1B \ \ \ \text{и они совпадают} \Longrightarrow AB=DC$$
