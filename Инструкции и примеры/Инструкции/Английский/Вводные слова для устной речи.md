## Reasons

| Given              | Given + noun                  | I decided to arrive a bit latter given his previous delays                     |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Given that         | Given that + clause           | Kate is absent today given that she has some problems                          |
| What with          | What with + noun              | It's been a really busy week, what with revising for exams                     |
| Seeing as/that     | Seeing as/that + clause       | I will not stay for long seeing as you are busy                                |
| In view of         | In view of + noun             | UN forces will withdraw in view of an achieved peace                           |
| Owing to           | Owing to + noun phrase        | Owing to weather, school closed for today                                      |
| Bearing in mind    | Bearing in mind + clause/noun | we shall deal with these in turn, bearing in mind the types                    |
| In the interest of | In the interest of + noun     | We supported that military assistance in the interest of our national security |

## Переход к теме

| As far as ___ are concerned | As far as my hobbies are concerned, I love hiking |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| When it comes to ___        | When It comes to cooking, I am still learning     |
| As for ___                  | As for movies, I prefer thrillers                 |
| As regards ___              | As regards hobbies, I love hiking                 |
| Regarding ___               | Regarding hobbies, I love hiking                  |

## Да, но

| Of course…, but      | Of course robots increase efficiency, but they also displace workers                           |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| It’s true that…, but | It’s true that robots make production cheaper, but the social cost may be too high             |
| While it’s true that | While it’s true that robots lower the cost of goods, they also risk creating mass unemployment |
